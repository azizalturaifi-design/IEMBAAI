How the system learns
Each time a planner accepts, modifies, or rejects an agent suggestion, Hermes captures the decision and its reasoning into lessons-learned.md. Offline, in a dreaming stage, openclaw replays that experience and distills recurring patterns into a candidate skill — a reusable rule with provenance. The system is probabilistic: a skill carries a confidence, stays a candidate (advisory only) until it clears an evidence bar, and every proposal is grounded in company-data records before a human promotes it to active. Skills can always be paused or retired. Learning is logged to the audit trail; the model is never retrained on a single decision.

How escalation works
Each notification is sent to a first responder with a response SLA. If they don't acknowledge in time, Atlas automatically escalates to the next person in the chain (e.g. owner → manager → committee), notifies them, and logs every hop to the audit trail. Nothing silently stalls.
